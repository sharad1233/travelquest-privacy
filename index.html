Privacy Policy

Travel Quest: Word Search

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Travel Quest: Word Search is a general-audience travel and word puzzle game designed for a broad range of players, including children, teenagers, adults, and older users. We respect your privacy and aim to keep data collection limited to what is needed to operate, improve, and support the game.

1. Information We Collect

Travel Quest: Word Search does not require you to create a developer account or provide your name, email address, phone number, home address, or other directly identifying information in order to play.

The app and third-party services used by the app may automatically process limited technical and usage information, such as app interactions, device and operating-system information, app version, gameplay events, diagnostics, advertising-related information where permitted, and similar information used to operate and improve the app.

2. Firebase Analytics

Travel Quest: Word Search uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the game. Analytics events may include actions such as opening the journey screen, selecting a city, starting or completing a level, using hints, participating in the Daily Challenge, and viewing or interacting with the Shop.

This information is used to understand feature usage, improve gameplay, diagnose technical problems, and improve the overall user experience. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, phone number, or home address to Firebase Analytics.

3. Advertising

Travel Quest: Word Search may display advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising-related information, app interactions, and diagnostic information to display ads, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

Where required by applicable law or platform policy, age-appropriate or restricted advertising treatment is used for younger users. Personalized advertising is not intended to be served to users who are treated as children under applicable law.

4. Children's Privacy

Travel Quest: Word Search is a general-audience game that may also be enjoyed by children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit their name, email address, phone number, home address, or similar directly identifying information through normal gameplay.

Where a user is identified or treated as a child under applicable law, we intend to apply age-appropriate privacy and advertising protections. Parents or guardians who believe that information relating to a child has been collected improperly may contact us using the contact information below.

5. Apple Game Center

Travel Quest: Word Search may use Apple Game Center for features such as leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is provided and managed by Apple. Information associated with a player's Game Center account is handled according to Apple's terms and privacy practices. Travel Quest: Word Search does not receive or store your Apple ID password.

6. In-App Purchases

Travel Quest: Word Search may offer in-app purchases using Apple's StoreKit system. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your full credit-card or payment-card information.

The app may receive transaction status and product information needed to unlock purchased content, remove ads, restore purchases, or deliver in-game rewards.

7. Game Progress and Local Data

Travel Quest: Word Search may store gameplay information on your device, including level progress, completed cities, Air Miles, hints, rewards, settings, achievements, Daily Challenge results, and other game state needed for the app to function.

Locally stored gameplay information may be removed if you delete the app from your device, subject to any information separately maintained by Apple or other third-party services.

8. Third-Party Services

Travel Quest: Word Search may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Apple Game Center
  • Apple StoreKit

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. How We Use Information

  • Operate and maintain Travel Quest: Word Search
  • Understand how players use the game
  • Improve levels, features, difficulty, and user experience
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Process and restore in-app purchases
  • Support leaderboards and Game Center features
  • Detect abuse, fraud, or technical misuse
  • Comply with applicable legal and platform requirements

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers such as Google and Apple where necessary to provide analytics, advertising, purchases, leaderboards, diagnostics, and related app functionality.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to the retention practices of the third-party services used by the app.

You can generally remove locally stored game data by deleting the app from your device. For privacy questions or deletion requests relating to information controlled directly by us, contact us using the email address below.

12. Privacy Choices

Depending on your country, age, device settings, and applicable law, you may have choices regarding advertising, tracking, consent, and data processing. Where required, Travel Quest: Word Search may present consent or age-related choices before certain advertising or data processing occurs.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date above will be revised.

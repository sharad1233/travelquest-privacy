Contact Support

If you are reporting an issue, please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and a short description of the problem. Screenshots are also helpful when possible.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased Explorer Pass or Remove Ads, open Travel Quest and use the Restore Purchases option available in the app.

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

In-App Purchases

Travel Quest may offer optional in-app purchases including Air Miles, Explorer Pass, and Remove Ads.

If an Apple payment was completed but the purchase is not reflected in the app, please try restoring purchases or contact us using the email address above.

Game Progress

Travel Quest may use Apple services such as iCloud, CloudKit, and Game Center for supported game progress, achievements, and leaderboard functionality.

Make sure you are signed in to the appropriate Apple services when using these features.

Daily Challenges & Notifications

Notifications are optional and may be used to remind you about Daily Challenges. You can change notification permissions at any time in your device Settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to play?
No. Travel Quest does not require a separate account or login.

Can I play without making purchases?
Yes. In-app purchases are optional.

How do I restore Explorer Pass or Remove Ads?
Use the Restore Purchases option in the app while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I report a bug?
Email us at travelquestws@gmail.com .
